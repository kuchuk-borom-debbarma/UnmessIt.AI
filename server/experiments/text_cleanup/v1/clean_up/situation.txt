# END GOAL
Transform messy, unstructured, chaotic brain-dumps (e.g., rushed journals, stream-of-consciousness thoughts) into clean, concise, standardized, and restructured text.

# KEY CAPABILITIES REQUIRED
- **Topic Restructuring:** Dynamically identify and group related concepts, even if they are scattered far apart in the original dirty text.
- **Pronoun Resolution:** Intelligently resolve ambiguous pronouns ("it", "he", "she", "they") to their explicit nouns, BUT leave them alone if resolution is uncertain (do not hallucinate).
- **Sanitization:** Strip out fillers, fix terrible grammar, and eliminate run-on sentences without losing the original meaning or irony.

# EXPECTED INPUT
- The absolute worst-case scenario.
- Decently huge blocks of text.
- Zero punctuation or paragraphs.
- Heavy use of fillers ("um", "like", "you know"), passive voice, and irony.

# CONSTRAINTS
- **Local Execution:** Must run entirely on local hardware.
- **Model Size:** Capped at relatively weak LLMs (~7B parameters at best, e.g., Qwen2.5 7B, Llama 3 8B). The pipeline/prompting strategy must be resilient enough that a weak model does not fail under the complexity.
